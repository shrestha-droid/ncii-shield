1Register a fingerprint

Images and videos are decoded and hashed in a Web Worker (dHash: shrink to 9×8 greyscale, one bit per neighbour comparison). Videos are sampled at 8 frames.

What would be sent: nothing yet. In production, only these hex strings reach the server, keyed to a random session secret kept in your browser.

2Watch the feed get matched

A simulated scraper ingests an upload every 3 s. Half are random; half are re-uploads of a registered fingerprint with 0 to 6 bits flipped, like a recompressed or resized copy. A BK-tree finds anything within Hamming distance 10.
